Photospace

A local-first depth image pipeline for the creative web.

Drop a photo, estimate depth in the browser (WebGPU/WASM), preview depth-aware effects, and export a .photospace package. Everything runs locally — nothing is uploaded.

Photo formats are bundled as available and listed in meta.json; the runtime tries them in order, with JPEG always included as the universal fallback. Extra maps are optional and declared in meta.json. All included maps share one resolution — if their combined PNG size exceeds the limit, they are rebaked at a smaller common resolution.
